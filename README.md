# Mod-7-team-project
#### Set up
You are a security guard named Jack, working at Senator Armstrong's Bar and Grill similar to chuckey cheese. However, the animatronics come alive at night and are trying to kill you. Your goal is to survive until 6AM whilst not dying to these animatronics.

#### Time
Each hour in game is roughly 1 minute in real life. Effectively, you need to survive for 6 minutes or die trying. 

#### Animatronics
There are 3 animatronics trying to kill you. There names are Murphy, Armstrong, Sundowner, and Blue Guy. All of them cannot move if you are staring at them and they may say some weird stuff when you look at them. They each move at different times, with the most opportunistic being Armstrong. 

#### The door
Your only protection is a door, which unfortunatly has limited power. The animatronics cannot enter through the door, but once the power goes out, you are screwed. To open or close the door, press W. The door does not take power while it is closed. 

#### Controls
You can only look through cameras set up in specific loactions, such as Hallway A or the senator containment. To change the camera, press A or D. On the opening voice screen, you can press Space to skip it. 

#### Resetting
The game must be reset by exiting the game
